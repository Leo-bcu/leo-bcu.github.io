abstract: >-
  A digital twin visualization platform for underground coal mine gas monitoring and multi-robot collaborative exploration. It renders billion-scale point clouds (Potree), overlays real-time gas concentration heatmaps (Three.js custom shaders), tracks the robot fleet with live sensor streams and alerting, and provides AI-assisted target identification and decision support. Built with React 18 + TypeScript + Vite on the frontend and FastAPI + Open3D on the backend.
